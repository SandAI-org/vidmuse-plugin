# Reviewed UGC creative cases

These are qualitative feedback from this skill's development on 2026-10-09,
not campaign results, proof of local-language quality, or guaranteed formulas.
Study the decisions and failure modes; do not reuse plots indiscriminately.

## Protein powder: 12-second fixed presentation — rejected

One trained male creator at a garage-gym table, a prefilled shaker, casual
dialogue about not washing a blender, short shaking action, no substantive
preparation or CTA. The user judged it dull, without a hook or desire to watch.

Lesson: natural wording, photorealism and small gestures do not create a
compelling concept. A prefilled cup does not fulfill a preparation demo. Do
not label a partial talking clip as a complete product-use advertisement.

## Protein powder: 30-second operator POV and demonstration — liked

Training detail starts the video. An offscreen friend asks about the creator's
physique; the creator responds, rises, introduces the actual product, shows
powder/preparation, drinks, and closes with CTA. The user called it very good.

Lesson: exercise creates visible interest; the question gives a reason for the
response; product use belongs to the routine; each change of view reveals new
information. The structure fits this concept, not every UGC. Do not imply the
product alone caused the physique. Fine packaging text and shaker-lid continuity
still had defects; creative approval does not certify every production detail.

## Sunglasses: 22-second clean-agent try-on — creatively weak

An independent agent, without chat history, selected a porch try-on with the
line "Wait. Same outfit. Watch this." It showed frame details, putting on the
actual glasses, the worn look and a CTA. User feedback: creative and hook were
ordinary, and opening camera movement felt unmotivated.

Lesson: a complete structure and correct wearing action prove production
competence, not creative strength. The promised transformation was predictable
and insufficiently specific. The opening instructions prescribed a full-body
hold then a step forward without a strong information reveal. Improve the
audience-relevant styling question, contrast or interaction; keep a stable
view when useful. This does not mean try-on must copy the protein interview.

## Adding cases

Record product, market, mode, opening, payoff, actual feedback and observed
limitations. Separate proposed concepts from generated results and user
acceptance from measured performance. Preserve useful negative examples as
well as approved ones; extract a transferable mechanism with its scope.
