# Sound Design

Sound is part of scene direction in Director mode. Dialogue remains primary;
music, texture, effects, and silence organize attention around it.

## Sound states

Give every act a sound state: source-only, sustained atmosphere, music bed,
rhythmic build, reduced near-silence, or resolved close. State changes follow
narrative energy rather than scene count.

## Event types

- Spatial sounds support camera movement.
- Causal sounds such as click, snap, lock, shutter, or tonal resolve support a
  visible action or completed proof.
- Material sounds support drawing, typing, paper, glass, or interface behavior.
- Silence creates room around a claim, proof, or transition.

Each substantial scene has at most one primary sound event plus supporting
ambience. More events require a clear hierarchy.

## Rules

- Do not attach a whoosh to every entrance.
- Do not let music mask consonants or product names.
- Do not fabricate or download media without authorization and provenance.
- Record music/SFX source, license state, timing, and gain with other media
  provenance.
- When assets are unavailable, degrade to dialogue and deliberate silence;
  keep the storyboard cue for a future authorized pass.

## Review

Review motion with sound, then once muted to ensure the picture still reads.
Review dialogue and music loudness across the full draft, not only isolated
scenes. Flag abrupt ambience cuts, repeated stock effects, excessive event
density, and a finale without sonic resolution.
