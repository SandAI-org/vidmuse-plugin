# Official Quality Benchmark

Use official HyperFrames launch work as a production benchmark, not a scene
template. Compare rendered evidence against the principles below.

## Proof

- Important lines receive visible evidence rather than decorated repetition.
- Source footage remains visible when it is the most honest proof.
- The opening demonstrates the output promise before explaining the system.

## Shot craft

- Each substantial scene has one focal point and one principal camera action.
- Motion develops after entry and exits into a designed handoff.
- Hard cuts and continuous transitions are chosen for narrative reasons.
- Word timing, holds, and silence feel intentional at delivery fps.

## Controlled plurality

- Acts may use different visual cultures, materials, and typography character.
- A film spine keeps those worlds in one production.
- Variety demonstrates narrative range rather than effect inventory.

## Integration

- Type, source footage, diagrams, generative art, 3D, and sound feel composed
  into the same film rather than stacked from unrelated demos.
- Official mechanisms no longer carry recognizable demo copy or styling.
- The signature sequence could not be reused by changing only the transcript.
- A signature mechanism changes narrative job at least once; it does not merely
  repeat one branded appearance or advertise an inventory of unrelated effects.
- Every developed state has one readable semantic owner. Outgoing source
  windows, labels, panels, and material surfaces clear before the successor
  owns the frame unless their coexistence is an intentional composite.
- Expensive shader, 3D, or generative scenes occupy a narratively privileged
  moment; they are not interchangeable spectacle between ordinary sentences.
- A simpler fallback exists for every GPU-heavy scene and preserves the proof,
  even if it deliberately gives up the material flourish.

## Completion quality

- Hero frames pass static craft review.
- Transition boundaries contain no black flash, early hide, or scale jump.
- Dialogue remains intelligible and primary sound events are motivated.
- The full film has an energy contour, quiet passages, a central proof, and a
  resolved ending (a flat contour is tells **T1/T4** in
  [packaging-tells.md](packaging-tells.md)).
- The rendered film clears the temporal tells: no metronome spacing, no
  single entrance grammar, exits authored, graphics living in the footage's
  space (**T1, T3, T5, T7**).
- Material findings from at least one full-film correction review are closed.

Record named gaps in `evaluation.json`. Do not average away a critical scene
failure with a high overall score. Differences caused by subject, authorized
assets, duration, or budget are acceptable only when documented.
