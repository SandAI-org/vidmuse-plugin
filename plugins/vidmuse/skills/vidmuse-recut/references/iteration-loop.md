# Rendered Iteration Loop

Director mode produces a strong first draft, then keeps working from rendered
evidence until explicit stop conditions pass.

## Passes

1. **Hero frames:** render the developed state of every substantial scene.
2. **Motion reel:** review camera weight, development, transitions, and sound.
3. **Act review:** review energy shape, density, repetition, and quiet passages.
4. **Full draft:** watch without pausing; record attention loss and structural
   problems before local polish.
5. **Correction review:** resolve every material finding. A no-change review is
   valid only when it records that no material issue was found.
6. **Final polish:** verify technical integrity, typography, subtitles, audio,
   media quality, and export metadata.

## Correction hierarchy

Correct in this order:

1. wrong narrative job or visual proof;
2. wrong source mode or act world;
3. weak composition or hierarchy;
4. weak camera development or handoff;
5. sound mismatch;
6. local timing, spacing, and finish.

This order prevents polishing a scene that should be replaced.

## Evidence

Every completed pass points to rendered files or frames. Findings have
severity, status, target, and evidence. Corrections record before, after, and
the render that proves the change. Scores may summarize a review but cannot
override a named critical failure.

## Completion

Use `official-quality-benchmark.md` plus project stop conditions. If an
external constraint ends work early, deliver and label the best verified
draft, report the exact open gaps, and do not call it a finished film.
